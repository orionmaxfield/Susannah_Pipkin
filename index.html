Common questions

Before you hit play

Is this podcast in line with Church teachings?

That’s the whole point. We’re active members of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, and we believe sexual intimacy in marriage is a sacred gift from God. We stay inside Church doctrine and avoid fringe takes. We aren’t an official Church publication, and we’ll always say so.

Who is this for?

Anyone! We focus on Latter-day Saints who want to learn more about healthy sexuality.

What is sex therapy, exactly?

Sex therapy is strictly talk therapy. A certified sex therapist helps individuals and couples with desire, pain, communication, trauma, pornography, and the dozens of other things that get in the way of a healthy sexual relationship. Nothing physical ever happens in a session.

Is listening the same as therapy?

No. The podcast is education and encouragement. If you’re struggling in a way that isn’t getting better, please reach out to a licensed therapist. We’ll point you toward good resources along the way.

Can I send in a question?

Yes, please do. Ask a question and we may answer it on a future episode. Questions are kept anonymous.

Have a question you’ve never felt safe asking?

Send it in anonymously. Your question might be exactly what another couple needs to hear answered.
